1.Who we are

Achso is made by Deniz Maral, an independent developer based in Switzerland. In this policy, “Achso”, “we” and “us” mean Deniz Maral. For the small amount of personal information we receive (described in Section 8), we are the controller under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

You can reach us at dnzmaral@gmail.com.

2.At a glance

  • Your progress, settings and searches stay on your iPhone. Nothing is synced and we cannot access any of it.
  • The pronunciation trainer turns your voice into text on your iPhone. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking code, no account, and we never sell or share your data.
  • The app goes online only to fetch new versions of its dictionary from Apple’s iCloud servers and, for Achso Pro, to ask the App Store what you own (Section 7).
  • Achso Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment or Apple Account details.
  • We only receive what you choose to send us, like a support email, plus reports from Apple that do not identify you.

3.What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Achso app for iPhone and iPad and to these web pages. It does not cover Apple’s services such as iCloud, the App Store, Siri voices and Translation, or websites you open from the app. Those have their own privacy policies.

4.Data stored on your device

  • Learning progress: the cards you have practised, your answers, when each card is due again, and your streak and daily goal.
  • Settings: appearance, voice, speech speed, the language of meanings, the reminder time and similar choices.
  • Recent searches in the dictionary.
  • The dictionary itself, and newer versions of it you download.

This data stays in Achso’s storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. It is not synced to iCloud or anywhere else, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

5.Microphone and speech recognition

The pronunciation trainer asks iOS for the microphone and for speech recognition the first time you use it. It listens only from your tap on the microphone until you tap Done or it stops. Apple’s speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer) turns what you said into text on your iPhone; Achso compares that text with the German you were asked to say and shows the result.

The audio and the text are not recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off both permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security; the rest of the app keeps working.

6.Reading aloud and translation

Words and sentences are read by the German voices installed on your iPhone, through Apple’s speech synthesis on the device. If you turn on Apple Translation for meanings, Apple’s Translation framework translates them on your iPhone. iOS may download a voice or a translation language from Apple when you ask for it; Apple’s privacy policy covers those downloads. We receive nothing from either feature.

7.When the app connects to the internet

Achso connects to the internet in one situation of its own:

  • Dictionary updates: when you open the app, it asks a CloudKit database we operate on Apple’s servers whether a newer version of the dictionary exists, and downloads it if so. The request tells Apple which dictionary version is current, along with the network information any download involves, such as your IP address. It contains nothing about your progress or what you searched, and we do not receive it. It works without an iCloud account.
  • Achso Pro: the free version includes one practice session a day; Achso Pro (monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) makes practice unlimited. It is sold through the App Store: Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription, and we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account details. The app asks Apple which plans exist and whether your account owns one, so it can unlock Pro, and keeps the answer on your iPhone so Pro also works offline.
  • Emails you send us: a contact link opens your email app with a message addressed to us. Only what you choose to send reaches us.

8.What we receive

  • Messages you send us, and whatever they contain, including your email address.
  • Reports from Apple about downloads, sales, subscriptions and refunds, grouped by product, country and date. They do not tell us who you are.
  • Crash reports and app usage statistics, only if you chose to share analytics with app developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. Apple provides these without identifying you.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, Apple’s CloudKit may give your Apple Account a random identifier for our app when it fetches the dictionary. It does not tell us your name, email address or Apple Account, and we cannot link it to you.
  • If you join a TestFlight beta, the details Apple shares under its TestFlight terms: the name and email address you were invited with, builds installed, sessions, crashes and any feedback you submit.
  • App Store ratings and reviews you post. Reviews are public and show the nickname you chose.

We do not run servers of our own that receive data from the app, and there is no Achso account.

9.What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or include advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t use your device’s advertising identifier.
  • We don’t sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
  • We don’t access your location, contacts, photos or camera. The microphone is used only as described in Section 5.
  • We don’t build profiles about you or use your data to train AI models.

10.Your controls

  • Turn the microphone, speech recognition and notifications on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off the daily reminder, speech and Apple Translation in Achso’s settings.
  • Cancel or change an Achso Pro subscription in your App Store account settings (Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions), or from Achso’s settings.
  • Remove everything on your device by deleting the app.

11.Why we may process personal information

Where the GDPR or a similar law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Providing the app and the plan you bought (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): unlocking Achso Pro and keeping that verdict on your device.
  • Our legitimate interest in answering your messages, fixing problems and understanding how the app performs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations, such as keeping sales records for tax and accounting (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Your consent to Apple for sharing crash reports and analytics with developers, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.

12.How long data is kept

  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app.
  • We keep support emails as long as we need them to help you and handle follow-up questions, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

13.International transfers

Apple, and the provider of our email account, operate worldwide and may process information in the United States and other countries. They use recognised safeguards for international transfers, such as adequacy decisions, the EU–U.S. and Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks and standard contractual clauses, as described in their privacy policies.

14.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. Your learning data is on your iPhone only, so you control it yourself; we cannot access it.

The personal information we hold is what reaches us directly, such as the emails you send. To make a request about it, write to dnzmaral@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request from the same email address. For information Apple holds about you, use privacy.apple.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU or EEA, the authority where you live or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office.

15.Children

Achso does not collect personal information from anyone through normal use, including children. We do not knowingly receive personal information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where they live. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

16.Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and data protection. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, so protect your device with a passcode and keep your Apple Account secure.

17.This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub does not give us those logs. The pages use no cookies, analytics, web fonts or scripts from other companies.

18.Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how your data is handled, we will also give notice in the app or on this page before it applies.
