About the data

Licence

The dictionary pack is a derived database licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). You may share and adapt it, also commercially, as long as you give credit and share your adaptations under the same licence. It is provided as is, without warranty.

Where it comes from

Wiktionary (English and German editions, through kaikki.org / wiktextract), by the Wiktionary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0: meanings, genders, plurals, case forms, conjugations, verb objects and translations.

Tatoeba, CC BY 2.0 FR: example sentences and their translations. Every sentence in the pack carries its Tatoeba number and author, and those of its translation.

OpenSubtitles corpus (OPUS), by P. Lison and J. Tiedemann: only word counts, used to rank words by how often they are used.

Achso’s own lists of verbs with prepositions and of two-way preposition sentences, CC BY-SA 4.0.

What was changed

Entries were extracted from Wiktionary, normalised, checked against usage counts and a German spelling dictionary, ranked by frequency in German subtitles, combined with Tatoeba sentences and the curated lists, and written into one file the app reads in place. Each release lists its sources in ATTRIBUTION.md.
