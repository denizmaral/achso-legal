Achso: questions, privacy and support Achso

Achso helps you with the hard parts of German: der, die, das, verbs with prepositions, cases, conjugation and haben or sein. It keeps what you learn on your iPhone. Here are straight answers, and the full policy behind them.

Common questions

Is Achso free?

Yes: every drill, one practice session a day, and the whole dictionary, always. Achso Pro makes practice unlimited, including the pronunciation trainer. It comes as a monthly or annual subscription, each with a free first week, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I cancel, or get Pro back on a new iPhone?

Cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or from Achso’s settings with Manage subscription; Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. To get Pro back after reinstalling or on a new device, tap Restore Purchases in Achso’s settings while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account, and every drill and the whole dictionary work offline. When you are online, the app checks for a newer version of its dictionary. Read more

What does Achso store, and where?

Your progress (which cards you know and when they are due), your streak, your settings and your recent searches. They stay on your iPhone and are deleted with the app. Nothing is synced, and we cannot see any of it. Read more

Why does Achso ask for the microphone?

Only for the pronunciation trainer. After you tap the microphone, Apple’s speech recognition turns what you say into text on your iPhone, and Achso compares it with the German you were asked to say. The audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Read more

Words are not read aloud.

Check the volume: Achso plays speech even with the mute switch on. For a better voice, install a German “Enhanced” or “Premium” voice in Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices > German, then choose it in Achso’s settings.

The pronunciation trainer does not react.

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Achso in Settings > Privacy & Security. The first use may download Apple’s German speech model.

Meanings appear in English, not in my language.

Achso shows Wiktionary’s meaning in your language when it has one. Turn on Apple Translation in Achso’s settings to fill the gaps; iOS may download the language first, and the translation runs on your iPhone.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete the app. Your progress is stored nowhere else, so this removes all of it.

Does Achso track me or show ads?

No ads, no analytics or tracking code, no advertising identifier, and we never sell or share your data. Read more

Where do the words come from?

From open data: Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0), example sentences from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR) and word frequencies counted over the OpenSubtitles corpus (OPUS). The app’s credits list the sources and the authors of every sentence.
